AB OUT
 
Nestled along the pristine coastline of
Karnataka, Gokarna is a charming
beach destination known for its
spiritual significance, scenic
landscapes, and laid-back atmosphere.
Unlike crowded commercial beach
towns, Gokarna offers a perfect blend
of peaceful temples, golden sandy
shores, dramatic cliffs, and
breathtaking sunsets.
From the sacred Mahabaleshwar
Temple and the vibrant streets of the
town to the picturesque beaches of
Om Beach, Kudle Beach, and Paradise
Beach, every corner of Gokarna offers
a unique experience.
The journey becomes even more
exciting with excursions to the mystical
Yana Caves, the serene backwaters of
Honnavar, and the iconic Murudeshwar
Temple overlooking the Arabian Sea.
Carefully planned for both relaxation
and exploration, this itinerary promises
an unforgettable coastal getaway filled
with nature, adventure, spirituality, and
memorable moments.

ARRIVAL IN GOKARNA
 Arrival at Gokarna Railway Station.
